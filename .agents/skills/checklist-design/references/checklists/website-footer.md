# Footer — Website

A footer is a persistent section at the bottom of a page that provides consistent access to secondary navigation, legal information and contact details to avoid cluttering the main content area.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/footer

## Items

### Size and responsiveness
Define footer width (full or contained)

### Style
Colours, font sizes, visual brand elements

_Tip: Making the footer notably contrast the body content helps users understand they’ve reached the footer_

### Content links
Visuals, states and destinations of all links

### Legal and compliance links
Pages like privacy policy, terms and other legal content are typically located here

### Logo or name of business
For brand consistency and as a link back to homepage

_Tip: You can also include a tagline or brief description to reinforce the core purpose_

### Hierarchy and layout
Footers should be balanced between logo, links and additional actions

_Tip: Group links into meaningful categories but only when there's a considerable amount and organisation would help_

### Social media
Logos and/or links to social media platforms you are active on

### Mobile app download actions (if applicable)
If product is a mobile app, including links to download at Apple or Google stores

### Regulatory and trust certifications
Badges or logos that show certification of a certain security or trust measure that has been enforced

### Back to top button
If your content is exceptionally long and you think the user is likely to want to return

### Contact methods
An email, phone number or form to complete for support

### Partner logos
A suitable place for partners to be shared so they are easily visible on every page

### Accepted payment methods (if applicable)
Relevant for any e-commerce experience
