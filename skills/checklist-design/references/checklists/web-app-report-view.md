# Report View — Web app

A read-only view of generated business data containing charts, tables, and summaries designed for review and sharing.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/web-app/report-view

## Items

### Report metadata
The title, author, date range, and when it was generated, providing enough context to make sense of the data outside the platform

### Summary or key findings section
A brief narrative or set of highlights at the top, before the detailed data

### Data visualisations
Charts, tables, and graphs that make the data easier to interpret

_Tip: Certain charts represent information in the clearest narrative e.g. line charts for trends, bar charts for comparisons_

### Filter capabilities
The ability to click into a chart or row to explore the underlying data in more detail

_Tip: Even read-only reports benefit from basic filtering, since narrowing to a single segment without regenerating the whole report saves significant time_

### Export to PDF, CSV, or spreadsheet
Download options for sharing or further analysis outside the platform

_Tip: PDF suits presentations, while CSV suits further analysis_

### Table of contents
Sections and headings laid out for readers to navigate to specific areas of the report if needed
